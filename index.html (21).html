Delivering Independent Engineering Excellence

MPS Engineering is a premier independent renewable energy engineering and advisory firm. Based in Australia, we specialize in mitigating project risks and maximizing asset value across the entire lifecycle of utility-scale Solar PV, Battery Energy Storage Systems (BESS), and complex Microgrid projects.

Who We Are

MPS Engineering is an independent engineering and advisory consultancy dedicated to advancing Australia's renewable energy infrastructure. We operate across a diverse geographical presence, delivering highly specialized engineering capabilities for the industries powering tomorrow.

Our Mission

To accelerate the transition to sustainable energy by providing world-class independent engineering, mitigating project risks, and maximizing asset value for our clients. We strive to be the definitive voice of technical truth in the renewable sector.

Our Vision

How We Work

We provide independent, technology-agnostic advice. Our methodology combines rigorous technical analysis with deep commercial acumen. Through a structured engineering process and specialist partnerships, we ensure every project is derisked from concept to operation.

Our Expertise

Comprehensive Engineering & Advisory Services

Delivering end-to-end solutions for Solar PV, BESS, and Microgrid projects across Australia.

Strategic & Commercial Advisory

Business strategy, market entry, commercialisation pathways, business model development, and stakeholder engagement for renewable energy projects and technology providers.

Technical Engineering & Design Advisory

Independent electrical engineering support for solar PV, battery energy storage systems (BESS), hybrid systems and microgrids including system configuration, technical specifications, Australian Standards compliance, and design review.

Project Development Support

Early-stage project scoping, feasibility assessment, technology selection, risk identification, and development pathway planning.

Procurement & Supply Chain Management

Procurement strategy, tender management, equipment specification, supplier evaluation, contract negotiation support, and supply chain risk assessment using established manufacturer relationships across Asia, Europe, and the United States.

Commercial & Financial Advisory

Project financial modelling, PPA structuring, wholesale and merchant revenue modelling, hybrid energy modelling, and commercial due diligence.

Delivered with specialist energy economist partners.

Grid Connection & Network Advisory

Grid connection strategy, application support, network studies coordination, and interface management with DNSPs and TNSPs.

Delivered with specialist network engineers.

Owner's Engineer & Independent Oversight

Independent technical representation for project owners including design review, EPC tender evaluation, construction monitoring, quality assurance, and commissioning oversight.

Remote, Off-Grid & First Nations Solutions

Specialist advisory for remote and Indigenous communities including microgrid and hybrid system design, cultural engagement support, funding application assistance, and reliable solar plus storage solutions.

Compliance, Standards & Certification

Guidance on Australian Standards, product certification requirements, warranty compliance, and regulatory pathways for PV and battery systems.

Technology Integration & Remote Monitoring

Advisory on integrating renewable generation with energy management systems and telecommunications for remote monitoring, reduced operational costs, predictive maintenance, and improved system reliability.
