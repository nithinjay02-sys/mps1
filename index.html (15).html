Renewable Energy Division

Solar Energy Solutions

Powering the Future, One Project at a Time

At Alcorn, we deliver complete turnkey solar power solutions—from megawatt-scale utility installations to compact rooftop systems for homes and businesses.

Introduction

Engineering sustainable solar infrastructure for a greener tomorrow.

Alcorn India's Renewable Energy Division specializes in the end-to-end execution of solar power projects. As a specialized EPC (Engineering, Procurement, and Construction) contractor, we bring unparalleled technical expertise, leveraging our deep background in electrical infrastructure to ensure every solar plant we build is highly efficient, seamlessly integrated with the grid, and engineered to last for decades.

Turnkey Solar Projects

We provide comprehensive EPC services across all scales of solar energy generation:

  • Utility-Scale Solar Parks: Ground-mounted megawatt (MW) scale installations.
  • Commercial & Industrial: High-efficiency rooftop systems optimizing unused factory space.
  • Residential Solutions: Compact, reliable systems for residential communities.

Detailed Engineering

Our in-house design team ensures maximum yield and structural integrity:

  • Comprehensive site surveys and 3D shading analysis.
  • Optimal array layout and PVsyst generation forecasting.
  • Custom structural engineering for high wind-load resistance.

Installation & Commissioning

Precision execution by veteran technicians:

  • Safe, code-compliant mounting of structures and modules.
  • Advanced string and central inverter integration.
  • Rigorous testing, grid synchronization, and handover.

Material Sourcing & Supply

Uncompromising quality through global supply chains:

  • Procurement of Tier-1 Mono PERC and Bifacial PV modules.
  • High-durability mounting structures and BOS (Balance of System) components.
  • In-house manufacturing of custom solar ACDB/DCDB electrical panels.

Why Alcorn for Solar?

Electrical DNA

Unlike pure solar installers, our deep background in substations and transmission lines means we handle grid-integration and high-voltage aspects flawlessly.

Single-Point Accountability

From concept and module procurement to commissioning and long-term O&M (Operations & Maintenance), you deal with one reliable partner.

International Presence

Taking Indian engineering excellence global. Alcorn is actively expanding its renewable footprint, executing high-yield solar infrastructure projects across Southeast Asia and the Middle East, adhering to stringent international quality and safety benchmarks.

The Design-to-Delivery Process

A systematic, quality-driven approach from blueprint to dispatch.

1. CAD Design

Custom drafting using advanced EPLAN and AutoCAD electrical software.

2. Fabrication

CNC punching, bending, and specialized 7-tank powder coating process.

3. Assembly

Precision busbar routing and integration of Tier-1 switchgear components.

4. Routine Testing

Rigorous Megger, HV, and functional testing before client dispatch.
